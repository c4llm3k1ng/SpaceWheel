1. Data Controller

Jan-Sören Ehmann

2. What Data We Collect

When you create an account and use the app, the following personal data is stored:

  • Username – freely chosen by you; publicly visible in leaderboards
  • Password – stored exclusively in encrypted (hashed) form; we never have access to your plaintext password
  • High score – your best game result
  • Friends list – internal user IDs of accepted friends
  • Friend code – a randomly generated code to identify you to other players
  • Pending friend requests – internal user IDs of players who have sent you a request

We do not collect location data, device identifiers, advertising data, or biometric data.

3. Purpose of Processing

  • Creating and managing your user account
  • Displaying global and friends-based leaderboards
  • Operating the friend system (sending, accepting, and declining requests)

4. Legal Basis

Processing is based on Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract), as the data is necessary to provide the game's features. For users outside the EU, processing is necessary to deliver the service you have requested.

5. Data Retention

Your data is stored for as long as your account exists. Upon account deletion, all personal data is permanently removed.

Delete your account: Send an email to janehmann97@web.de with the subject "Delete account" and your username. Deletion is completed within 30 days.

6. Third-Party Services – Google Firebase

Space Wheel uses Google Firebase (Google LLC, 1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA) for the following services:

  • Firebase Authentication – for account management and login
  • Cloud Firestore – for storing user profiles, high scores, and the friend system

Your data is processed on Google's servers, which may be located outside the European Union (in particular the United States). This transfer is safeguarded by Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) pursuant to Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR.

Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
Firebase Privacy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

No personal data is shared with any other third parties.

7. No Advertising, No Tracking, No Cookies

Space Wheel contains no advertising, no tracking technologies, and uses no cookies. No user profiles for marketing purposes are created.

8. Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • Access (Art. 15 GDPR) – request information about the data we hold about you
  • Rectification (Art. 16 GDPR) – correction of inaccurate data
  • Erasure (Art. 17 GDPR) – deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restriction (Art. 18 GDPR) – restriction of processing
  • Data portability (Art. 20 GDPR) – receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection (Art. 21 GDPR) – object to the processing of your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at janehmann97@web.de.

9. Right to Lodge a Complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. If you are located in the EU, the competent authority is typically that of your country of residence. In Germany, a list of authorities can be found at the Federal Commissioner for Data Protection (BfDI).

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update this privacy policy as needed. The date of the most recent change is shown above. Users will be informed of material changes.

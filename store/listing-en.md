## Full description (max. 4000 characters)

```
Space Wheel is a reaction game with a simple idea and a steep learning curve: four coloured balls orbit a centre. Bars with gaps fall from above — and only the ball in the matching colour may pass through. One swipe turns the wheel. One mistake ends the run.

HOW IT WORKS
Swipe across the screen to turn the wheel. You start with a single ball. As your score grows, cyan, yellow and purple join in, until you have to keep four colours in view at once. New obstacles keep arriving as well: double bars, triple gaps, striped zebra bars for several colours, and bombs you have to avoid entirely.

RISKY BEATS SAFE
Pass close to the edge of a gap and you score double. Several riskys in a row unlock bonus tiers — from Triple Risky through Magical Risky to hefty streak bonuses at 25, 50, 100 and more hits in a row. Playing safe scores points. Playing risky sets records.

SIX ITEMS THAT CHANGE EVERYTHING
Star Immunity – invulnerable for a short while
Slow Motion – everything falls more slowly
Shield – absorbs one hit
Demolition – your ball shoots down the next bars
Wide Gaps – more room to get through
Colour Swap – opposite balls trade places

SIX WORLDS
The higher your score, the further you travel. From deep space with its distant spiral galaxy, across an ice planet with northern lights, a desert planet with dunes and an emerald world full of crystals, to the ringed planet and the volcano world with flowing lava and erupting craters. Every world has its own soundtrack, which grows fuller with each new ball colour — and on the final stage it switches to a faster finale with a heartbeat rhythm. In the menu you can try out each world beforehand, or pick one to keep for your real games.

PLAYABLE WITH COLOUR VISION DEFICIENCY
Besides the standard view there are two tested colour sets: one for red-green deficiency and one for blue-yellow deficiency. You switch them in the settings, and along with the colours every text in the game, in the help and in the tutorial changes too.

PLAY AGAINST FRIENDS
Save your highscore to an account, add friends by username or by six-digit friend code, and compare yourself on the global leaderboard and the friends leaderboard.

A TUTORIAL YOU PLAY
The tutorial does not just explain, you try out every step right away. Controls, bar types, items and scoring — each in a protected practice round where your real run stays untouched. A help page in the menu keeps all the rules available to look up.

NO ADS, NO PURCHASES
No ads, no in-app purchases, no tracking. Space Wheel can be played entirely without an account and works without an internet connection — your highscore is synced the next time you start with a connection.
```
